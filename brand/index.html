Prompt Arena

Score the prompt, not the student.

01
Concept

A chevron caught inside a ring. The chevron is the universal symbol of the prompt — every command line begins with it. The ring is the arena: the bounded space where the prompt is judged. The orange-to-cyan gradient runs through the chevron from input to verdict, mechanical to holistic, and ties the identity to the wider QAME family.

02
The mark

Four expressions, one form.

Geometry: outer circle r=88, stroke 8. Chevron M 64 56 L 134 100 L 64 144, stroke 14, linejoin=round. Drawn on a 200×200 grid.

03

The serif voice carries the "judgment" half of the brand — editorial, considered, slightly ceremonial. Pairs with the chevron mark, which carries the "input" half. Tracking is set tight (letter-spacing: -0.01em) for display sizes; revert to default at 18pt and below.

04
Lockups

Horizontal and vertical.

Prompt Arena
Prompt Arena

In the horizontal lockup, the wordmark cap-height aligns to the mark's vertical centre. Spacing between mark and wordmark equals one chevron-width. The vertical lockup centres on the mark's vertical axis; spacing equals two chevron-widths.

Orange and cyan are the QAME family signature, used here only as accents — never as flat backgrounds covering more than a thin stripe or pill. The page itself is paper on ink. Keep the palette restrained: when in doubt, use one accent on neutral, never both at once except in the gradient itself.

DM Serif Display is reserved for moments of weight — page titles, the editor's verdict, scenario openings. Plex Sans is the workhorse for everything in between. Plex Mono signals "this is data or technical metadata": filenames, hex codes, dimensions, schema previews.
